About

A proposed non-interest microfinance bank for everyday work in Nigeria.

Alzas is being built for households, market traders and small businesses. The aim is savings, payments and financing on contracts you can read.

Licensing is still in progress. This page is not an offer to open an account or provide financing.
What we hold to

A real contract

Each product must be a genuine sale, lease or partnership. Not an interest loan under another name.

A price you can read

Cost, profit, rent and fees are shown before you agree. A partnership is not sold as a guaranteed return.

Goods you can point to

Trader stock and household assets start as named items, not an open cash amount.

People first

Built with women-owned businesses, young traders, rural households and people with a thin credit file in mind.

Where we are

Alzas is proposed from SHM Complex, Mabushi, Abuja. The first services, once approved, are accounts, payments and Murabaha for named goods.
