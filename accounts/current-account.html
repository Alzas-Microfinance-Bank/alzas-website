Current account

Day-to-day banking for work and home. Pay suppliers, receive payments, and transfer funds with fees and rules set out upfront. Non-interest.
The Alzas current account supports everyday banking: deposits, withdrawals, outgoing payments, and customer collections. It is offered on approved non-interest terms—we do not charge or pay interest on balances.

Who is it for?

This account suits salaried workers, traders, and small businesses that need a reliable place for daily money movement. Cooperatives and associations can also use one account with named signatories.

  • People who trade in markets or shops
  • Small and medium businesses
  • Households that need day-to-day payments
  • Cooperatives, market groups, and associations

What you can do

Manage the inflows and outflows from your business or household in one place.

  • Pay suppliers, workers, and bills
  • Receive money from customers
  • Send transfers to other people or accounts
  • Collect payments through approved Alzas channels
  • Keep track of your balance and activity

Clear fees and rules

We charge only the fees permitted for this account and disclose them before you sign. We confirm who may operate the account, who must sign, and how withdrawals work. Records are maintained and reviewed under our controls.

No interest

This is a non-interest account. Balances are not used for interest-based lending. Any charges are disclosed service or transaction fees.

How to open an account

Visit a branch, speak with a field officer, or use our digital channel when it is available. Bring valid ID and the documents required for your business or group. You receive a product summary with fees and rules before you agree.
