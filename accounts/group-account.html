Group account

One account for your cooperative or association. Named signatories, dual-control safeguards, and rules your members can rely on. Non-interest.
The Alzas group account is a shared non-interest account for cooperatives, market associations, and savings groups. Members pool funds with authorised signatories and controls that match how your group actually decides and pays.

Who is it for?

This account suits registered or well-governed groups that need one bank relationship for collections, contributions, and shared expenses—not individual day-to-day wallets for every member.

  • Cooperatives and thrift societies
  • Market associations and trader unions
  • Savings groups with written bylaws
  • Community and faith-based associations managing shared funds

What you can do

Run group money with signatory control and a clear record of who approved each movement.

  • Receive member contributions and levies
  • Pay suppliers, venues, and shared costs
  • Assign multiple signatories with dual-control where required
  • Manage member-related payments under your agreed rules
  • Review statements and audit trails for the group

Signatories, fees, and governance

We set out who may open, operate, and close the account before you sign. We explain signatory limits, dispute steps, and how a member can exit fairly. Permitted fees are disclosed up front. Records are maintained and reviewed under our controls.

No interest

This is a non-interest account. We do not charge or pay interest on balances. Any charges are disclosed service or transaction fees.

How to open an account

Visit a branch or speak with a field officer. Bring your group’s registration or bylaws, valid IDs for signatories, and a resolution naming who may sign. You receive a product summary with fees, signatory rules, and governance terms before you agree.
