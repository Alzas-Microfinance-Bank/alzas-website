Savings account

Set money aside for what comes next. Build reserves on non-interest terms—with clear rules and no guaranteed return promised.
The Alzas savings account helps households, salary earners, and traders keep financial reserves in a secure, compliant place. You can add to your balance regularly and withdraw when you need liquidity. We do not charge or pay interest, and we do not advertise a guaranteed return.

Who is it for?

This account suits anyone who wants a dependable place to save outside day-to-day spending—whether you earn a salary, run a stall, or set aside trading profit for slower months.

  • Households building an emergency buffer
  • Salary earners saving from each payday
  • Traders and shop owners reserving stock money
  • Anyone preparing for school fees, rent, or equipment

What you can do

Keep reserves separate from your current account while staying able to reach your money when the terms allow.

  • Make regular deposits at branch or through approved channels
  • Withdraw when you need funds, subject to account rules
  • Track balance and activity
  • Pair with a current account for everyday payments
  • Move toward a named goal with clear target and timeline

Clear fees and rules

We disclose permitted fees before you sign. We explain minimum balances, withdrawal limits, and any charges that apply. Records are maintained and reviewed under our controls.

No interest, no guaranteed return

This is a non-interest savings account. We do not pay profit or interest on balances in the interest-based sense, and we do not promise a fixed return. Any benefit outside principal is only where an approved product structure allows it, and only as stated in your agreement.

How to open an account

Visit a branch, speak with a field officer, or use our digital channel when it is available. Bring valid ID and any documents we require. You receive a product summary with fees and rules before you agree.
