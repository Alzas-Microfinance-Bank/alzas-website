Goal savings

Save toward something specific—school fees, equipment, or a business asset. A named target, a clear date, and terms you see before you commit.
Goal savings is a structured non-interest arrangement that helps you reach a defined target by an agreed date. You know what you are saving for, how much you aim to set aside, and what happens if your plans change—all before you sign.

Who is it for?

This suits households, traders, and small businesses that already know what they are working toward and want discipline without vague promises about returns.

  • Parents saving for school fees or term costs
  • Traders building toward stock or a stall upgrade
  • Artisans saving for tools or small equipment
  • Small businesses reserving for a named purchase

What you can do

Follow a savings plan tied to a goal you name at the start.

  • Set a target amount and target date in your agreement
  • Deposit on a rhythm that matches your income
  • Track progress toward the goal in your statements
  • Withdraw or adjust only as your product terms allow
  • Use funds for the stated purpose when the goal is met

Clear terms and fees

We disclose fees, early-access rules, and what happens if you miss a deposit before you sign. There are no hidden charges dressed up as something else. Records are maintained and reviewed under our controls.

No interest, no guaranteed return

Goal savings is non-interest. We do not promise a fixed profit rate on your plan. Any benefit beyond principal applies only where an approved structure allows it, and only as written in your agreement.

How to start

Visit a branch, speak with a field officer, or use our digital channel when it is available. Tell us your goal, amount, and timeline. Bring valid ID and any documents we require. You receive a product summary before you agree.
