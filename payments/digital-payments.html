Payments

Digital payments

Send and receive money on approved Alzas channels—for your household, shop, or group. Fees and limits are disclosed; human support backs the digital service.
Digital payments connect your Alzas account to approved mobile and electronic channels so you can transfer funds, pay people, and receive money without queuing at a branch for every transaction. The service is offered under authorised payment arrangements (including agency structures such as Wakalah where applicable).

Who is it for?

Anyone with an eligible Alzas account who needs convenient payments—not only smartphone users, but also merchants and groups that want reconciled digital flows.

  • Households paying bills and sending money to family
  • Traders receiving customer payments
  • Small businesses paying suppliers and staff
  • Groups with authorised signatories making approved transfers

What you can do

Use digital channels that Alzas has approved and can support with records you can check.

  • Send transfers to other accounts or approved beneficiaries
  • Receive inbound payments where the channel allows
  • See transaction history and balances through agreed reporting
  • Link digital use to your current or group account
  • Get help from branch or field staff when something fails

Fees, limits, and security

We disclose transaction fees, daily limits, and cut-off times before you use the service. You are responsible for safeguarding PINs and devices; we explain how to report fraud or errors promptly.

Availability

Channels go live only where licensing and technical readiness allow. We do not claim nationwide instant access before that is true. When a channel is proposed, terms are in writing first.

How to start

Open or use an eligible Alzas account, then speak with us to enrol on digital channels when they are available for your profile. Bring valid ID and any documents we require.

Speak with Alzas or contact us if you have questions.
