Merchant collections

Receive regular payments from customers through Alzas—with simple reports so you can see what came in, when, and from which channel.
Merchant collections is for businesses and groups that need a dependable way to take money from customers—daily sales, subscriptions, levies, or recurring dues. Funds settle into your Alzas account under agreed rules, with transaction reporting to support your records.

Who is it for?

Merchants and organisations that collect often and want less cash-handling risk, plus clearer reconciliation at the end of the day or month.

  • Market traders and shopkeepers
  • Subscription or membership businesses
  • Associations collecting member dues
  • Service providers with repeat customer payments

What you can do

Collect through approved Alzas channels that fit your customer base.

  • Accept customer payments into your merchant arrangement
  • View collections summaries and transaction detail
  • Integrate with digital payments where available
  • Separate collection flows from personal household money when needed
  • Escalate disputes or failed payments through Alzas support

Fees and settlement

Collection fees, settlement timing, and chargeback or reversal rules are disclosed in your merchant agreement. We do not hide per-transaction costs in marketing copy—you see them before you sign.

Honest records

You remain responsible for prices, receipts to customers, and tax obligations where they apply. Alzas provides the banking and collection rails; we do not replace your sales records.

How to apply

Speak with Alzas with proof of your business or association, expected volumes, and how customers pay today. You receive written terms before collections go live.
