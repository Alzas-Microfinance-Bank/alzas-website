Support fund

Limited Qard Hasan assistance when you face genuine hardship. No financing profit—funding is ring-fenced and separate from commercial finance products.
The support fund is a benevolent facility, not a product we sell for profit. Under Qard Hasan, eligible customers may receive a small amount to bridge an emergency or social-impact need. You repay principal as agreed; Alzas does not charge a financing profit on the support itself. The fund is funded and governed separately from Murabaha and other commercial lines.

Who may qualify?

Eligibility is narrow and transparent. We assess genuine need against published criteria—not every shortfall qualifies, and capacity limits apply.

  • Customers in documented emergency hardship
  • Situations the fund charter explicitly covers
  • Applicants who can show how they will repay principal
  • Cases reviewed under Shariah and social governance rules

How Qard Hasan differs

This is not trader stock, home goods, or any Murabaha sale.

  • No profit margin on the support amount
  • Repayment is principal only, on terms stated upfront
  • Ring-fenced pool—commercial deposits are not mixed blindly with charity capital
  • Separate approval track from commercial credit

What we do not do

We do not use coercive collection on hardship cases. We do not market the fund as easy cash for business stock or lifestyle spending. Misrepresentation can disqualify an application.

How to enquire

Speak with a field officer or contact customer care. Explain your situation honestly; bring ID and any evidence the criteria require. A decision may take time while the case is reviewed—you receive written terms if support is offered.
