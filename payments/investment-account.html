Investment account

A Mudarabah arrangement: you provide capital, Alzas manages eligible activities, and actual profit is shared at an agreed ratio. This is investment—not a savings account with a promised return.
The investment account is for customers who understand that profit and loss are real. Under Mudarabah, you contribute funds; Alzas invests or deploys them within an approved mandate. Profit is shared as written in the contract. If activity loses money, loss is allocated according to the same contract—not hidden or guaranteed away.

Who is it for?

Individuals or businesses seeking Shariah-compliant participation in profit—not a fixed “interest rate” product.

  • Savers with surplus funds and investment horizon
  • Small business owners diversifying idle balances
  • Customers who read and accept risk disclosures
  • Those who do not need instant access to every naira

How Mudarabah works

Capital plus management—terms are specific to the mandate you sign.

  • You place capital under the agreed investment mandate
  • Alzas manages within approved, disclosed activities
  • Actual profit is shared at the ratio in your contract
  • Loss, if it occurs, is borne as the contract states
  • Reporting follows the schedule in your agreement

No guaranteed return

We do not promise a fixed profit rate or capital protection. “Shariah-compliant” does not mean low risk. Past performance, if ever shown, is not a promise of future results.

Not a substitute for savings

Emergency money and short-term bills usually belong in savings or current accounts with clear liquidity rules. Investment capital can go up or down.

How to apply

Speak with Alzas after you understand the risk disclosure. Bring valid ID, source-of-funds information we require, and questions you want answered in writing. The mandate and profit-sharing ratio are documented before you commit.
