About Alzas

A non-interest bank for everyday needs and small business.

Alzas Microfinance Bank is a non-interest (Islamic) bank in Nigeria. We help households, traders and small businesses save money, send and receive payments, and buy goods they need.

We do not charge interest. We use clear sale, lease and partnership contracts. You always see the full price before you agree.
What we stand for

Real contracts

Every product uses a real, written contract. We buy, sell, lease or partner with you. We do not hide an interest loan behind another name.

Clear prices

We show the full price, fees, payment dates and who owns the goods before you sign. No hidden charges.

Open to more people

We serve traders, women-owned businesses, young entrepreneurs, farmers, and people who do not yet have a long bank record.

Shariah review

Our Advisory Committee of Experts (ACE) reviews and approves our products. We follow Central Bank of Nigeria rules for non-interest microfinance banks.

Our vision and mission

Vision. To be a trusted non-interest microfinance bank. We want more people in Nigeria to save safely and grow honest work.

Mission. To offer savings, payments and finance with clear, approved contracts. We protect customers, manage risk with care, and keep strong Shariah governance.

How we work with you.

Simple steps. Plain words. You always know what you are agreeing to.
1. Ask
Visit a branch, speak to a field officer, or use our digital channel.
2. Understand
We give you a simple product sheet: total price, fees, who owns the goods, and when you pay.
3. Apply
Send your application with your ID and proof of your work or business.
4. Check
We look at what you can afford, your cash flow, the supplier, and the goods. We do not look at collateral only.
5. Sign
You sign the right contract at the right time. The words are clear.
6. Receive
You receive the goods or service. Your payment plan is confirmed in writing.
7. Support
Pay as agreed. Call us if you have a question or a complaint. We will answer fairly and on time.
Why no interest?

You know the total price

The price is fixed and shown before you agree. It does not grow like interest on a loan.

Real goods, real work

Each deal is tied to real goods or a real business. Not just cash with extra charges.

Fair late rules

If you pay late, extra charges follow a written policy. We do not keep adding interest on interest.

Scholars review the products

Qualified Shariah experts on our Advisory Committee of Experts review our contracts before we offer them.
