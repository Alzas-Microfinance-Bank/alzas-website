## Services

### 1. Accounts

#### Alzas Current Account
A day-to-day transaction account for individuals, traders, SMEs and groups. Manage payments, collections and cash flow with no interest charged or earned.

- For: Individuals, traders, businesses, groups
- Features: Deposits, withdrawals, transfers, payment collection

#### Alzas Savings Account
A non-interest savings account that helps you build financial reserves safely. No misleading return promises — just a secure, compliant place for your money.

- For: Households, salary earners, traders
- Features: Regular deposits, goal tracking, liquidity on demand

#### Group / Association Account
A shared account for cooperatives, market associations and savings groups, with authorised signatories and dual-control safeguards.

- For: Cooperatives, market associations, savings groups
- Features: Multi-signatory access, member payment management, dispute and exit process

#### Goal-Based Savings
A structured savings arrangement that helps you save toward a specific target — school fees, equipment, a business asset — with clear terms and no hidden charges.

- For: Households, traders, small businesses
- Features: Target amount, defined timeline, transparent terms

---

### 2. Trade & Asset Finance

#### Trader Stock Finance (Murabaha)
Alzas purchases identified stock from your supplier and sells it to you at a disclosed, fixed total price with an agreed repayment schedule. No interest — just a transparent sale.

- For: Market traders, retailers, distributors
- Structure: Murabaha (cost-disclosed sale)
- How it works: You identify the goods and supplier → Alzas buys from the supplier → Alzas sells to you at a fixed price → you repay in instalments

#### Household Asset Purchase (Murabaha)
Finance for identified household goods — appliances, furniture, electronics — purchased by Alzas and sold to you at a fixed, disclosed price with a manageable repayment schedule.

- For: Households, salary earners
- Structure: Murabaha
- How it works: You identify the item → Alzas buys it → Alzas sells it to you → you pay over an agreed period

#### SME Goods Finance (Murabaha)
Finance for eligible inventory and raw materials for small and medium businesses. Alzas buys the goods and sells them to you at a disclosed profit — no cash disbursal, no hidden charges.

- For: Small and medium enterprises
- Structure: Murabaha
- Purpose: Finance productive activity without cash lending

#### Equipment Finance (Murabaha / Ijara)
Finance or lease tools, machinery, refrigeration, generators and commercial assets. Depending on your preference and use, structured either as a sale (Murabaha) or a lease (Ijara).

- For: Micro and small enterprises
- Structure: Murabaha or Ijara
- Features: Asset register maintained; full ownership and maintenance terms disclosed

#### Farm Input Finance (Murabaha)
Finance for seeds, fertilisers, agrochemicals and farming equipment to support planting cycles and agricultural productivity. Repayment schedules are structured around actual harvest/income cycles.

- For: Farmers and agricultural groups
- Structure: Murabaha
- Features: Seasonal repayment schedule; supplier network

#### Supplier / Purchase Order Finance
Finance for small suppliers and contractors to fulfil verified purchase orders. Alzas pays the supplier directly against confirmed orders and invoices.

- For: Small suppliers, contractors
- Structure: Murabaha or Istisna (as appropriate)
- Features: Invoice verification, supplier payment workflow, delivery confirmation

---

### 3. Leasing

#### Equipment Leasing (Ijara)
Alzas purchases and owns the asset, then leases it to you for an agreed rent and period. Maintenance responsibilities, insurance arrangements and end-of-lease terms are clearly defined in the contract.

- For: Businesses needing equipment without full ownership upfront
- Structure: Ijara (lease)
- Assets: Machinery, tools, refrigeration, generators, commercial equipment
- Features: Alzas retains ownership risk; rent begins when asset is available

---

### 4. Investment Accounts

#### Mudarabah Investment Account
You provide the capital; Alzas manages eligible investment activities. Actual profit generated is shared between you and Alzas at an agreed ratio. This is a genuine investment — no guaranteed return is promised.

- For: Individuals and businesses seeking Shariah-compliant investment
- Structure: Mudarabah (capital + management)
- Key terms: Profit shared by agreed ratio; loss allocation follows the contract; investment mandate disclosed upfront

#### Musharakah Enterprise Finance
A genuine partnership where both you and Alzas contribute capital to a business activity. Profit and loss are shared in proportion to capital contribution and the agreed structure.

- For: Viable businesses seeking a finance partner
- Structure: Musharakah (joint venture / partnership)
- Features: Transparent governance, financial reporting, partner due diligence

---

### 5. Agricultural Finance

#### Salam Agricultural Finance
Alzas pays the full purchase price in advance for clearly specified goods (crop type, grade, quantity, delivery date and location) to be delivered at harvest. Gives farmers upfront liquidity while locking in a future sale.

- For: Farmers and agricultural cooperatives
- Structure: Salam (advance purchase)
- Features: Full advance payment; delivery specifications clearly defined; storage and logistics separately arranged

---

### 6. Payments & Collections

#### Digital Payments & Collections
Send and receive money, collect payments from customers and manage transactions through Alzas's approved digital channels. Available to individuals, merchants and groups.

- For: Retail customers, merchants, associations
- Structure: Wakalah (agency) or approved payment arrangement
- Features: Mobile/digital access, merchant collections, reconciliation support

#### Merchant Collection Service
A dedicated collection service for businesses that need to receive regular payments from customers — market traders, associations, subscription businesses.

- For: Merchants, traders, associations
- Features: Recurring fee income, transaction reporting, digital integration

---

### 7. Social Support

#### Qard Hasan Support Fund
A benevolent, no-profit loan available for limited emergency or social-impact situations, funded and operated separately from commercial products. No financing profit is charged.

- For: Eligible individuals in genuine hardship
- Structure: Qard Hasan (benevolent loan)
- Note: Ring-fenced funding; transparent eligibility criteria

3. **Apply** — Submit your application with ID and business evidence

6. **Receive** — Asset or service delivered; payment schedule confirmed in writing

## Why Non-Interest Finance?

Alzas operates on contracts of sale, lease and partnership — not loans with interest. This means:

- You always know the **total price** before you agree to anything
- There is **no compounding** if you fall behind — late charges follow a defined, transparent policy
- Every transaction is backed by a **real asset or genuine business activity**
- Our contracts are **reviewed and approved** by qualified Shariah scholars (ACE)

---

## Shariah Compliance

All Alzas products are structured under approved Islamic finance contracts and reviewed by our **Advisory Committee of Experts (ACE)** before launch. We operate under the CBN's regulatory framework for **Non-Interest Microfinance Banks (NIMFBs)** in Nigeria.

Contract types used:

| Contract | Plain Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Murabaha** | Alzas buys the asset and sells it to you at a disclosed, fixed total price |
| **Ijara** | Alzas owns and leases an asset to you at agreed rent |
| **Mudarabah** | You provide capital; Alzas manages; profit shared by agreed ratio |
| **Musharakah** | Joint venture — both parties contribute capital and share profit/loss |
| **Salam** | Alzas pays in full upfront for goods you will deliver at a future date |
| **Wakalah** | Alzas acts as your agent for payments and collections |
| **Qard Hasan** | A benevolent loan with no profit charged |
