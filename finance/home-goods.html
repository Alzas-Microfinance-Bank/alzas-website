Home goods

Named household items—appliances, furniture, electronics—at a total price you see before you sign. Alzas buys, then sells to you under Murabaha with an agreed payment schedule.
Home goods finance helps households and salary earners replace or upgrade essential items without an interest-based loan. You choose the specific product; Alzas purchases it and sells it to you at a fixed, disclosed price. You repay over a period that fits your budget, as set out in the contract.

Who is it for?

This product suits people who need a particular item for the home and want the full cost and payment plan in writing first.

  • Households buying appliances or furniture
  • Salary earners spreading the cost of electronics
  • Families replacing worn or broken home equipment
  • Renters furnishing a new home within agreed limits

How it works

Murabaha: a sale with a disclosed total price, not a generic cash loan.

  • You identify the item, seller, and price
  • Alzas buys the goods from the seller
  • Alzas sells the item to you at an agreed total price
  • You pay instalments on the dates in your contract; delivery and ownership follow the terms

What you see before you sign

Item description, total price, fees, instalment amounts, and dates are documented before you commit. We explain what happens if you need to pay early or if a payment is late.

Not an interest loan

The margin is part of the disclosed sale price under approved non-interest rules. We do not quote a separate interest rate or promise that this is “cheaper than a bank loan” without the full written price.

How to apply

Visit a branch or speak with a field officer. Bring valid ID, proof of income where required, and details of the item and seller. You receive a product summary before you agree.
