Farm inputs

Finance named seeds, fertiliser, agrochemicals, and small farm tools through Murabaha—with a repayment rhythm tied to your planting and harvest cycle, not a generic loan schedule.
Farm input finance helps farmers and agricultural groups buy what they need for the season. You identify inputs and suppliers; Alzas purchases the goods and sells them to you at a disclosed total price. Instalments are structured around realistic harvest or income timing, as agreed before you sign.

Who is it for?

This product suits growers who can name the inputs, the season, and how sale of produce will support repayment.

  • Smallholder farmers
  • Farmer groups and cooperatives
  • Horticulture and vegetable growers
  • Livestock keepers buying feed and related inputs where eligible

How it works

Murabaha on identified agricultural inputs—not cash lending with interest.

  • You specify inputs, quantities, and approved suppliers
  • Alzas buys from the supplier
  • Alzas sells the inputs to you at a disclosed total price
  • You repay on a schedule aligned to harvest or agreed cashflow

What you see before you sign

Input list, total price, fees, payment dates, and what happens if harvest is weaker than expected are documented up front. We work with realistic seasonal assumptions—we do not promise outcomes from the weather or market.

Not an interest loan

Cost is in the disclosed sale price. There is no interest rate quoted on the side. Farm finance carries real agricultural risk; the contract states how that risk is shared—it is not eliminated.

How to apply

Speak with a field officer or visit a branch. Bring valid ID, farm or group details, and supplier quotations. You receive a product summary before you agree.
