Tools and equipment

Get the tools, machinery, or generators your work needs—by purchase (Murabaha) or lease (Ijara). Ownership, rent, maintenance, and end-of-term rules are written in the contract.
Tools and equipment finance supports micro and small enterprises that need productive assets without an interest-based loan. Depending on the asset and how you will use it, we structure the arrangement as a disclosed sale (Murabaha) or a lease (Ijara). An asset register is maintained and the contract states who owns the equipment, who maintains it, and what happens at the end of the term.

Who is it for?

This product suits businesses and artisans whose income depends on reliable equipment—and who can explain how the asset supports repayment.

  • Workshops and repair businesses
  • Food vendors with refrigeration or processing tools
  • Small manufacturers and processors
  • Enterprises needing generators or commercial machinery

How it works

We match the contract to the asset. You choose the path that fits before you sign.

  • Murabaha: You name the asset; Alzas buys and sells it to you at a disclosed total price with instalments.
  • Ijara: Alzas owns the asset and leases it to you for agreed rent over a set period; maintenance and insurance are defined in the lease.

What you see before you sign

Asset description, total price or rent, payment dates, insurance expectations, maintenance duties, and transfer or return rules at the end of the term. No hidden fees dressed up as something else.

Not an interest loan

Murabaha uses a disclosed sale price. Ijara uses rent for use of an asset Alzas owns. Neither is structured as an interest loan, and we do not guarantee that every asset qualifies—we assess each case.

How to apply

Visit a branch or speak with a field officer. Bring valid ID, proof of your business, and quotations for the equipment. You receive a product summary before you agree.
