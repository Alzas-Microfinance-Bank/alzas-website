Trader stock

Restock your shop with named goods from your supplier. Alzas buys, then sells to you at a disclosed total price with an agreed payment plan—Murabaha, not an interest loan.
Trader stock finance helps market traders, retailers, and distributors refill inventory when cash is tight. You identify the stock and supplier; Alzas purchases the goods and sells them to you at a fixed, written price. You repay in instalments on the schedule you agree to before signing.

Who is it for?

This product suits traders who need identifiable stock to sell and can show how they will repay from trading income.

  • Market traders and stall owners
  • Retail shopkeepers
  • Small distributors with named suppliers
  • Seasonal traders restocking for busy periods

How it works

This is a Murabaha (cost-disclosed sale), not a cash loan with interest.

  • You name the goods, quantity, and supplier
  • Alzas buys from the supplier
  • Alzas sells the stock to you at a disclosed total price
  • You repay in agreed instalments; ownership and delivery follow the contract

What you see before you sign

Total price, fees, payment dates, and what happens if you miss a payment are set out in writing. We do not hide charges in the price. We check that the goods match what you described.

Not an interest loan

Profit is built into the disclosed sale price under Murabaha rules. There is no separate interest rate. If the structure does not fit, we will say so—we do not dress up interest as something else.

How to apply

Speak with a field officer or visit a branch. Bring valid ID, proof of your trade, supplier details, and a stock list. You receive a product summary with the full price and schedule before you agree.
