How finance works

We do not lend cash with interest. We buy goods and sell them to you, lease an asset, or share a real business. You see the full price and rules before you sign.
Why no interest?

You see the total price

The price is written down before you agree. It does not grow like interest on a loan.

Real goods, real work

Each deal is tied to named goods or a real business. Not cash with extra charges on the side.

Clear late rules

If you pay late, extra charges follow a written policy. We do not keep adding interest on interest.

Scholars review the products

Our Advisory Committee of Experts reviews contracts before we offer them. That does not mean the deal has no risk.
Stock is a named list of goods, not a cash amount.

A sale of named goods.

This contract is called Murabaha. You name the goods and the supplier. Alzas buys those goods, then sells the same goods to you.

You see the cost and the profit as one total price. You pay on dates that match your work. When the sale is done, the goods are yours.

A sale is not a lease.

Sometimes Alzas owns the machine and you pay rent. That is Ijara. The contract says who looks after the asset and what happens at the end.

A partnership is different again. You put in money. We manage it or we both put in money. Profit is shared only if there is profit. Loss follows the contract. We never promise a guaranteed return.

What we offer, and how we work.

Alzas offers accounts, finance, payments, and support on non-interest contracts. We buy and sell named goods, lease assets, or share a real business. We do not lend cash with interest.

You see the full price, fees, and who owns what before you sign. We check what you can pay. We do not promise a guaranteed return. If you have a problem, you can complain and get a fair answer.
