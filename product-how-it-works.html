Stock for the shop, step by step.

This contract is called Murabaha. Alzas buys the goods you have identified, from a real supplier. The bank then sells those same goods to you.

You see the purchase cost and the agreed profit as one sale price. You pay that total on dates that follow your market cycle. Once the sale is complete, the goods are yours.
