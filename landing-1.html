How Alzas is different

A real contract

Each product follows a documented sale, lease or partnership. It is not an interest loan under another name.

A price you can read

You see the total sale price, rent or fee, and the payment dates, before you agree.

Goods you can point to

Trader stock and household assets are named items the bank buys and sells, or leases, under the contract.

A person as well as a screen

Digital payments where authorised, with field support where a trader’s cash cycle needs a conversation.

Trader stock finance, clearly explained.

For trader stock, Murabaha means Alzas buys the named goods and sells them to you at a disclosed cost plus an agreed profit. You pay that total over an agreed schedule. The goods are identified before the sale.

First for the shop, then for the home.

The first financing Alzas plans to offer is trader stock and household assets, both as Murabaha purchases of identified goods. Equipment leasing comes only after the bank can look after the asset.
